Zoo Year's Eve at Perth Zoo

An early NYE for little ones, with a 7:30pm 'midnight' countdown so everyone is home for bedtime.

What's included

  • Extended zoo opening hours
  • Live family entertainment on the Main Lawn
  • Kids' countdown at 7:30pm
  • Picnic or buy food at the café

About Zoo Year's Eve at Perth Zoo

Zoo Year's Eve at Perth Zoo is at Perth Zoo, 20 Labouchere Rd in South Perth (South of the River). It's one of the family-friendly options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Perth. Fireworks: No fireworks. Age: All ages.
